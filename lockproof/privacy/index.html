Lockproof Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Lockproof (“the app”) helps you photograph things like your door lock or stove when you leave home, so you can check them later. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Your photos and checklist

Photos you take in Lockproof, your checklist and your check history are stored only on your device. We have no servers, no accounts and no way to see them. Checks older than your history period (7 or 90 days) are deleted automatically. Deleting the app deletes all of this data.

Camera

The camera is used only when you start a check, to take the photos you choose to take.

Location (optional)

If you turn on “Alert when I leave home”, Lockproof saves the location of your home on your device and asks the system to tell the app when your phone leaves that area, so it can show you a reminder. This needs location access set to “Always”. Your location is used only on your device: it is never sent to us or to anyone else, and turning the alert off stops it. You can also change location access anytime in your phone's Settings.

Notifications (optional)

Lockproof can show local reminders (the leaving-home alert and an optional daily reminder). They are created on your device; no data is sent to a server for them.

Advertising

Lockproof is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address (used to estimate approximate location), and information about ad interactions, to show and measure ads. On iOS, ads are personalized only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, you're asked for consent through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice anytime under Settings → Ad privacy choices.

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

Children

Lockproof is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Cooldown Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Cooldown (“the app”) helps you wait before making impulse purchases. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Your list

The items you add (names, prices, links, photos) and your savings totals are stored only on your device. We have no servers and no accounts, and the app never connects to your bank or shops. Deleting the app deletes all of this data.

Camera and photos

Camera and photo library access are used only when you choose to attach a picture to an item.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules a local reminder on your device for when a cooldown ends. No data is sent anywhere to do this.

Advertising

Cooldown is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address (used to estimate approximate location), and information about ad interactions, to show and measure ads. On iOS, ads are personalized only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, you're asked for consent through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice anytime under Settings → Ad privacy choices.

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

Children

Cooldown is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
